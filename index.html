Who I Am

Alexander
Rolon

I'm a campus missionary with Resonate Fort Collins, reaching college students at Colorado State University with the gospel of Jesus Christ. I believe the college years are among the most spiritually formative of a person's life — and one of the most underreached seasons in the church today.

"The church that reaches the campus today shapes the world of tomorrow."

Resonate is a movement of collegiate church planting — communities of students who worship, disciple, and multiply. My role is to build that community at CSU: reaching new students, training leaders, and planting a church that will outlast my time here.

The Problem

The Church Is Losing a Generation

Millions of young people walk away from faith during their college years. Campuses are the most spiritually influential environments in the world — and largely unreached.

  • Over 60% of students who grew up in church leave during college
  • Most US campuses have little to no gospel-centered presence
  • The average age of conversion is 18 — prime college years
  • Churches are aging and shrinking without young leaders

The Opportunity

Campuses Are the Mission Field

A student reached today becomes a leader, parent, teacher, or pastor who carries the gospel for the next 60 years. The return on campus ministry is generational.

  • Students are in a season of deep spiritual openness
  • One campus can send graduates to every city and nation
  • Resonate has a proven model of church planting on campuses
  • Fort Collins and CSU are a gateway to the Mountain West
